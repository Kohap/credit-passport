## What to steal (not copy)

1. **Lido** — one job per view; primary CTA weight; quiet secondary links.
2. **Aave** — clear asset/status readouts; connected wallet as utility, not hero.
3. **Spark** — section titles + numeric hierarchy; trust via layout, not purple glow.
